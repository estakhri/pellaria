Capitolo II · Il gesto

A hand leaves
its signature.

The smallest decisions are the ones you feel for years: the resistance of a clasp, the turn of an edge, the rhythm of a saddle stitch.

Full-grain leathers are selected for character, not uniformity, so natural marks become part of the piece rather than something to hide.

Each pattern is positioned to follow the strength and direction of the hide, balancing structure with a natural hand.

Edges are refined in repeated passes, hardware is tested by hand, and every seam is inspected before the piece leaves the atelier.

From Rome, slowly

The city is
our last.

Built for long afternoons, stone stairways, train platforms and dinners that begin later than planned. This is Italian leather for life as it is lived.
